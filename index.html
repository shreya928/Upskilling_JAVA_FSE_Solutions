Community Bulletin

The city council has announced a series of exciting community events for the upcoming season. Residents are encouraged to participate in various activities ranging from cultural performances to sporting events.

The annual Music Festival will be held at Central Park this year, featuring over 20 local and regional artists. Entry is free for children under 12 and senior citizens.

A new cooking workshop series has been introduced for homemakers and young adults interested in learning traditional and modern cuisines from local chefs.

Sports enthusiasts can look forward to the City Sports Day, which includes track events, team sports, and fun activities for all age groups.

Cultural Heritage Night returns with folk dances, art installations, and traditional food stalls showcasing the diverse culture of our city's communities.

Registration for all events is now open through this portal. Seats are limited, so early registration is strongly recommended for popular events.
